Example (2025, problem 3)

Let $\N$ denote the set of positive integers. A function $f : \N \to \N$ is said to be $\textit{bonza}$ if

$$ f(a) \ \text{ divides }\ b^a - f(b)^{f(a)} $$

for all positive integers $a$ and $b$.

Determine the smallest real constant $c$ such that $f(n) \leq c n$ for all bonza functions $f$ and all positive integers $n$.
